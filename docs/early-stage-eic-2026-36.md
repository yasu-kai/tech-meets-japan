## Original discovery architecture
Discovery documents are separate from: (1) unique legal entity, (2) R&D project, (3) underlying invention, (4) products, (5) collaboration hypotheses and (6) private outreach records. Multiple projects may point to one entity; a project may have several participants. Funding awards do not prove current funding round, commercial readiness, Japan absence or demand for a local distributor.

## Source routes newly validated
- EIC selection lists: selected tech SMEs; company and research project pairing.
- CORDIS https://cordis.europa.eu/projects: EU project/participant data downloadable as CSV, JSON, XML; enable project->organization linkage.
- UKRI Gateway to Research https://gtr.gtr.ukri.org/: project, award, researcher, organization, outcome search and programmatic REST API.
- Innovate UK https://www.ukri.org/what-we-do/what-we-have-funded/innovate-uk/: historic award disclosure.
- EIC Fund https://eic.ec.europa.eu/eic-fund_en: portfolio, but EIC-backed firms can be late-stage; validate stage.

## Audit gates
Sources directly from agencies; date every release; preserve original identifiers; never conflate research project and trading company. Check exact organization identity, independent technical evidence, company site, Japan presence, eligibility, source coverage, duplication, business development demand, and original analysis before public publication. Source categories should not drive editorial subject selection. No outreach conducted.
