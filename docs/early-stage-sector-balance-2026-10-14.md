## Field audit protocol
Use one primary sector per company for concentration calculations, plus secondary tags. Deduplicate against all repository longlists by normalized company name, website domain, and known aliases; review ambiguous collisions manually. Track primary sectors, geography, source program, latest funding round, Japan presence, maturity, confidence, last-reviewed date, and verified partner solicitation separately. The 15% per-sector target is a sourcing heuristic, not a reason to ignore genuinely relevant firms.
