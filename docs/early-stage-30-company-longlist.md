## Qualification workflow
1. Find company's official site and dated product/research demo, and confirm it is the same legal entity.
2. Establish current maturity and funding stage; record source, publication date and confidence. An institutional list alone does not demonstrate a financing stage.
3. Verify Japanese subsidiary, representative, existing partners, distribution and patents; unknown stays unknown.
4. Write Japan-specific collaboration hypothesis and identify at least two credible potential Japanese partner organizations (hypotheses only).
5. Search official calls for partners; absent direct evidence, label collaboration interest **unconfirmed**.
6. Prioritize publication only after source-backed editorial audit; keep outreach/contact data in access-controlled CRM.

## Funnel
Target 30 researched and contacted → 9 replies → 4 needs confirmed → 2 qualified opportunities. All values are hypotheses, not outcomes.
